About TechNova

TechNova is our flagship annual technical and cultural fest bringing together over 5,000 students from 100+ colleges across the country. Designed to spark innovation and showcase extraordinary talent, TechNova offers an immersive platform to compete, learn, and collaborate.

This year's theme, "Beyond Horizons," focuses on artificial intelligence, green technology, and artistic expression.

Fest Highlights:

  • 24-Hour Hackathon with cash prizes worth $10,000
  • Keynote sessions by industry leaders and tech innovators
  • Battle of the Bands and Pro-Nite Cultural Showcase
  • Esports tournament & Robotics Arena
